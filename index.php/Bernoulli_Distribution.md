## Bernoulli Distribution
This is a Discrete [Distribution](Distribution) of [Random Variable](Random_Variable)s

Bernoulli trial: There are only two possible outcomes: success or failure 

\mu = p 
\sigma = \sqrt{p \cdot (1 - p)}
