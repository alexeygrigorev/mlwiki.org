## Joint Distribution

https://www.google.fr/search?q=joint+vs+conditional+distribution

For a categorical variable the distribution is given by the frequencies (relative frequencies) of the observations. 

The *joint distribution* of two categorical variables is the frequency of the observations for the 2 variables, considered as a combination
