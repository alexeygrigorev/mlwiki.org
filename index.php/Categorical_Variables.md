## Categorical Variables

Tabular
Frequency Tables - for distribution of one variable
Contingency Tables - for showing the joint distribution 

Graphical (Plot)
Bar Plots - to display single categorical variable
Stacked (Segmented) Bar Plot - to display several 

## Factors
- A categorical explanatory variable in an experiment is a *factor*
- The values of a factor are its *levels* 
- A particular combination of values for the factors is called a *treatment*

Example: 
- factor 1: $A$, $B$
- factor 2: $X$, $Y$
- 4 treatments: $A X$, $A Y$, $B X$, $X Y$

- *Experimental unit* - the smallest using to which a treatment is applied, 
- and a *block* - is a group of similar experimental units
