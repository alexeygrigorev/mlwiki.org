## Natural Language Processing

**Natural Language Processing** (NLP) is a field at the intersection of computer science and linguistics that studies how computers can process and analyze natural language text

Typical NLP problems and tasks include

- [Tokenization](Tokenization) and [Text Normalization](Text_Normalization) - splitting text into words and bringing them to a canonical form
  - [Stemming](Stemming) and [Stop Words](Stop_Words) removal
- [Part-of-Speech Tagging](POS_Tagging) - assigning grammatical categories to words
- [Word Sense Disambiguation](Word_Sense_Disambiguation) - assigning meanings to ambiguous words
- [Spelling Correction](Spelling_Correction)
- [Collocation Extraction](Collocation_Extraction) and term extraction
- information retrieval - see [Information Retrieval](Information_Retrieval) and [Text Mining](Text_Mining)
- topic modeling - see [Topic Modeling](Topic_Modeling) and [Latent Semantic Analysis](Latent_Semantic_Analysis)

A typical [NLP Pipeline](NLP_Pipeline) consists of several processing steps applied one after another.
