## Triangular Matrices
Triangular matrices 

Unit-triangular matrices have ones on the diagonal 

Properties 
- Inverse of upper (lower) is upper (lower)
- Product of two upper (lower) matrices is upper (lower)
- Inverse of unit upper (lower) is also unit upper (lower)
- Product of two unit upper (lower) matrices is also unit upper (lower)
