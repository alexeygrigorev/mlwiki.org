## Quantitative [Variables](Types_of_Variables)
*Quantitative Variables* (or *Numerical Variables*) are numerical values 

The following are applicable for them 
- min and max
- medians and quartiles
- mean and std
- (all from [Summary Statistics](Summary_Statistics))

And we could create
- [Boxplot](Boxplot)s, [Piechart](Piechart)s, tables

Scatterplot
